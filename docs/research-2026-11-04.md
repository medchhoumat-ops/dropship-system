# Product research – 4 Nov 2026 (US market, CJ US warehouse)

Sources: CJ API (`product/listV2?countryCode=US`, `product/query`, `stock/queryByVid`,
`logistic/freightCalculate`), Semrush (US database), Amazon US page 1 via Apify.
Fees assumed: PayPal-only store ≈ 9% + $0.49. Price = min(landed × 4–5, Amazon median).
Ad cost = Semrush CPC ÷ conversion rate (1.5% new store, 2.5% good store).

## Conclusions
1. With a PayPal-only checkout and paid Google Ads, **items under ~$30 retail rarely pay for their
   ads**. Profit per order must exceed CPC ÷ conversion rate.
2. Winners must have: Amazon median ≥ $25, landed ≤ $11, CPC ≤ $0.45, single variant, US stock ≥ 100.
3. Ways to make ads affordable: free Merchant Center listings first; bundles/2-packs to raise
   profit per order; Standard Shopping with manual CPC caps; kill a product after spending
   1× break-even CPA without a sale.
