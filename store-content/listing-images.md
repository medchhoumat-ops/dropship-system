# Product listing images – plan (FR market)

What the leading French stores in this niche do (from the competitor analysis in `docs/competitors-fr.md`),
and what Lumaube does better.

## What competitors show
- 6–10 images per product: one white/neutral studio shot, 2–3 bedroom lifestyle shots, 2–4 supplier
  infographics with feature icons, a size image, sometimes a "gift" image.
- Text on images is usually short French labels with icons (often machine-translated from the supplier),
  plus big promises ("-35 %", "85 % des utilisateurs…", fake review counts).
- Page order: gallery → title + stars → struck-through price → "livraison offerte" → add to cart → benefit
  bullets → long description → reviews → related products. SEO comes from blog/guide pages, not the product page.
